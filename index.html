The premise

Strong manipulation is not a single perfect action. It is a continuous loop of perception → action → feedback → recovery.

Most manipulation benchmarks reward getting the first move right. Mosaic-M asks a harder question: can your policy keep going when objects slip, goals shift, and observations arrive late?

03 / Evaluation

Reward the
second move.

Mosaic-M turns adaptation into a first-class metric.

Beyond binary success, we report time-to-recovery, intervention cost, robustness across perturbations, and how gracefully a policy degrades. The result is a behavioral fingerprint — not just a leaderboard number.
